About

Kushaagra Giriwar is a Bollywood and semi-classical vocalist based in the Delhi NCR region, performing solo, duo and full-band sets across weddings, college fests and private events. Notable performances include Pianoman (TPM) and multiple college fest stages.

Known for reading a room and shaping the setlist on the fly, Kushaagra blends chart-topping Bollywood hits with timeless classics to keep any crowd — from college fests to intimate celebrations — singing along till the very last song.

Also known to survive three encores, zero sleep, and one too many "bhaiya ek gaana aur" requests from the front row.
